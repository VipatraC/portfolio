Music · Analytics

Data Visualization · EDA

Spotify Streaming Analysis

What makes a song popular? I explored Spotify track data to find which audio features and genres are linked to streaming success.

  • Approach: compared audio profiles of high- vs. low-popularity tracks, popularity by genre and year, and feature correlations.
  • Finding: higher-energy, more danceable tracks tend to be more popular, and pop and hip-hop dominate.
  • Impact: informs playlist curation, marketing, and artist development.

Finance · Risk

Finance · Risk Modeling

Credit Risk Modeling & Simulation

A credit rating model inspired by Moody's and S&P, built to estimate how likely companies in each rating group are to default.

  • Approach: scored firms on ROA, leverage, liquidity, coverage and efficiency using 2015–2017 statements, then combined them with weights.
  • Output: ratings from A to CCC, followed by Monte Carlo simulation of default probability per rating group.
  • Impact: the kind of framework lenders use to decide who to lend to and at what price.

TT Operations · Profitability

Supply Chain · Data Analysis

Trojan Toys Shipment & Profitability Analysis

An analysis of a global toy manufacturer's shipment records, tracing how factories, markets and regions drive volume and revenue.

  • Approach: aggregated shipments by factory, market, date and weekday, then joined manufacturing cost, transport cost and market markup tables.
  • Output: revenue by factory–market pair, regional revenue (e.g. Europe), and each factory's lowest-revenue markets.
  • Impact: shows which factory–market routes carry the business and which are underperforming.
